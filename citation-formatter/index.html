How to Avoid Plagiarism with Proper Citations

Plagiarism is the unattributed use of someone else's words or ideas. Academic institutions treat it as a serious integrity violation, yet many cases stem from ignorance of citation rules rather than deliberate misconduct.

APA 7th vs APA 6th — What Changed?

APA 7th (2020) brought key updates: up to 20 authors listed (vs. 6 + et al. in APA 6th), DOI formatted as a hyperlink, removal of publisher location, and "Running head" no longer required for student papers. Always confirm which edition your institution requires.

APA vs MLA vs Harvard vs Chicago vs IEEE

APA 7th/6th — social sciences, psychology, education. MLA — humanities, literature. Harvard — UK universities, business. Chicago — history, some humanities. IEEE — engineering, computer science.

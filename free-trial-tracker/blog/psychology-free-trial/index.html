The Psychology of Forgotten Trials

Subscription companies know exactly what they're doing. Free trials are not a gift — they're a conversion strategy. By lowering the barrier to entry with a 7-day or 14-day trial, they bank on user inertia: most people will forget to cancel and silently convert to paying customers.

Free Trial Tracker disrupts this pattern. By displaying a high-visibility countdown for every active trial, the expiration date transforms from a hidden calendar entry into an unavoidable, real-time alert on your dashboard. The red-zone animation triggers when a trial has less than 48 hours remaining — impossible to miss.

All data lives in your browser's localStorage. No email address required. No server storage. No analytics on what services you use. This is the anti-Mint: a tool that respects your privacy completely.

How accurate is the countdown?

The tool calculates difference between your system clock and the expiry time down to the minute. Keep your device time accurate for best results.

Will I get a browser notification?

Not currently. To avoid background tracking permissions, this tool is designed to be checked as a daily or weekly habit — open it, scan the dashboard, act.

What happens when a trial expires?

The card shows "Expired" with a dimmed appearance. You can delete it once you've confirmed cancellation or transitioned to a paid plan.

How many trials can I track?

Unlimited. localStorage has ample capacity. Track dozens of trials simultaneously with no performance impact.

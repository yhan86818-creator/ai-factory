    🔥 About Incinerator

    Incinerator is a privacy-first subscription manager that runs entirely in your browser. No cloud, no account, no personal data collection. Everything stays on your device — always.

    Your data is never sent to a server. Ever. The name says it all: burn the waste, keep the savings.

    📋 How to Use

    1. Click "Add Subscription" and enter details
    2. Monthly & yearly costs calculate automatically
    3. Click 🔥 Burn to mark a service as cancelled
    4. Export JSON/CSV to back up your data
    5. Filter by category to spot overspending

    ❓ FAQ

    Where is my data stored?
    Entirely in your browser's localStorage. Nothing goes to any server.
    Can I use this on mobile?
    Yes — it works on iPhone, Android, and any modern browser.
    What happens if I clear my browser?
    Export a JSON backup first. Use Settings → Export JSON.
    Is this really free?
    Core features are free forever. Pro unlocks unlimited subs and advanced exports.

    Why Most People Overpay for Subscriptions

    The average person pays for 10+ subscriptions — and actively uses fewer than half. Services like Netflix, Spotify, Adobe CC, and a dozen SaaS tools pile up on credit cards with no visible total. Incinerator makes the cost visible. Once you see the number, you act.

    Subscription creep is real: you sign up for a free trial, forget to cancel, and pay for 6 months before noticing. Incinerator shows renewal dates at a glance so you're never caught off guard again.

    Privacy-First Design — No Exceptions

    Every other subscription tracker wants your email, your bank login, or your cloud account. Incinerator stores everything locally. Open DevTools → Network tab right now — you'll see zero outbound requests for your subscription data. That's the promise, and you can verify it yourself.

The "Streaming Only" Problem

We live in the era of streaming fragmentation. What used to be one cable bill is now 5, 10, or 15 different monthly subscriptions. Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max, YouTube Premium — it's easy to lose track of the cumulative cost until you're spending $150+/month on content alone.

Streaming Only is a specialized auditor for video entertainment. It lets you quickly calculate the annual impact of your "entertainment tax" and — crucially — factor in Family Plan Splits. If you share an account with 3 people, your personal burn rate is different from the invoice total. Enter the number of members per service and get your actual share instantly.

Can I add custom streaming services?

Yes. Use the "Custom" preset button to add any service not in the presets. Enter the service name and monthly price.

How does the family plan split work?

For each service, input the number of members sharing the plan. Your share = total monthly price ÷ members. The "Your Share" total sums these divisions across all services.

Does this connect to Netflix or any streaming account?

No. This is a manual auditing tool. No API connections, no account linking, no data transmission. Enter your subscriptions manually to maintain 100% privacy.

What does "Send to Incinerator" do?

It opens Incinerator — the full subscription audit suite — so you can do a comprehensive review of all your services, not just streaming ones.

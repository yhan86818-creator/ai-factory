Quick Start Guides

How to Merge PDFs

  1. Drop multiple PDFs into the Merge tab
  2. Reorder with ↑↓ arrows
  3. Click "Merge PDFs"
  4. Download instantly — zero upload

Compress Large PDFs

  1. Open the Compress tab
  2. Drop your PDF
  3. Choose compression level
  4. See exact size reduction before downloading

Extract Specific Pages

  1. Open the Split tab
  2. Upload your PDF
  3. Enter page ranges: "1-5, 7, 9-12"
  4. Get your extracted PDF instantly

Why Browser-Only PDF Processing Matters

When you upload a PDF to a cloud tool, sensitive documents — contracts, financial records, legal briefs — traverse the internet and land on servers where security controls may be inadequate or opaque.
